# Labelling rules (written before running any model)

Question: "If I held this stock, is this headline good, bad or neither for me?"

- positive: earnings/revenue beat, upgrade, price target raised, new deal/contract, strong growth, buyback, dividend raise
- negative: miss, downgrade, lawsuit/fine, layoffs due to weakness, recall, guidance cut, stock falls
- neutral: facts with no clear direction, previews ("X to report earnings"), lists, explainers, mixed news

Tie-breakers:
- Unsure → neutral
- Mixed good + bad → neutral
- Label for the ticker in the row, not the market overall
- Judge the headline only; don't look up the article or the price move

Note: these rules were decided before labelling, but this file was committed after the FinBERT run.
